Summary of Core Privacy Commitments

ReceiptVault is built on an uncompromising on-device privacy architecture. All text recognition (OCR) executes 100% locally on your phone using Google ML Kit. Receipt photos and financial totals are stored in your device's private sandbox storage. 2. Data Processing Architecture: Local vs. Transmitted

Google Play Developer Program Policies require clear technical transparency regarding data handling boundaries. ReceiptVault maintains three strictly delineated tiers of processing:

A. Local On-Device Processing (Zero Server Transmission)

The vast majority of app functionality occurs exclusively inside your Android device's hardware sandbox:

  • Receipt Camera Capture & Document Scanning: Images captured through CameraX or Google Play Services Document Scanner are saved directly to internal app storage (context.filesDir/receipts).
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Handled locally by Google ML Kit on-device models. Receipt image bytes never leave device RAM during text extraction.
  • Expense Database: Merchant names, line items, taxes, totals, and dates are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database managed by Android Jetpack Room.
  • App Lock Security: Security PINs are hashed locally with a salted SHA-256 digest. Biometric authentication is handled by Android's hardware-backed BiometricPrompt; raw biometric data is never accessible to the app.

B. User-Controlled Optional Cloud Sync (Google Drive)

If you choose to link your personal Google account for automated or manual backups, structured metadata is transmitted directly between your device and Google's servers over secure HTTPS/TLS. Crucially, receipt images are NEVER included in cloud backups.

C. Third-Party Network Services

ReceiptVault integrates select Google Play ecosystem SDKs for monetization and licensing: Google Play Billing (for lifetime Pro purchase verification) and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob banner ads for free users).

4. Google Drive Backup & Cloud Privacy

ReceiptVault includes an optional cloud backup and restore mechanism that integrates with your personal Google Drive account. The exact technical parameters of this integration are:

  • Strictly Optional: Google Drive backup is disabled by default. The app functions completely offline without connecting a Google account.
  • Restricted Scope (drive.file): ReceiptVault requests authorization strictly for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. Under Google's API security model, this permission grants ReceiptVault access only to the files and folders created by ReceiptVault itself. ReceiptVault has zero technical ability to read, view, modify, or list any other documents, sheets, or photos in your Google Drive.
  • No Receipt Photos in Backups: To maximize privacy and minimize bandwidth usage, ReceiptVault backups contain only structured expense metadata (merchant name, transaction date, line items, subtotal, tax, category, notes, and currency). Receipt photo files are permanently excluded from cloud synchronization.
  • Dedicated App Folder: Backup snapshots are written directly to a dedicated folder in your personal Drive titled ReceiptVault/backups.
  • Three Rolling Snapshots: ReceiptVault maintains a maximum of three rolling backup points. When a fourth backup is generated, the oldest snapshot is purged automatically.
  • SHA-256 Data Integrity: Every backup payload is hashed using SHA-256 before transmission and verified upon restoration to prevent data tampering or corruption.
  • User Revocation: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in ReceiptVault Settings, or revoke app permissions globally at Google Account Security Permissions.

5. Camera Sensor & On-Device Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

Receipt scanning is powered by Android CameraX, the Google Play Services Document Scanner API, and Google ML Kit Text Recognition (Latin script):

  • On-Device Computation: Text extraction runs entirely on your device's CPU/GPU/NPU using local ML Kit models downloaded to your device through Google Play Services.
  • Zero Cloud Inference: Receipt image bytes are never streamed, uploaded, or transmitted to any external AI endpoint, LLM server, or cloud vision API.
  • Local Review: Extracted fields (merchant, total, tax, items) are presented to you in a Review screen where you can edit, correct, or delete any data before saving it to the local database.

8. Calendar Reminder Integration

ReceiptVault allows you to set optional calendar reminders (such as 1-year warranty expiration or 30-day review reminders):

  • System Intent Mechanism: Reminders are created using Android's standard Intent.ACTION_INSERT directed to CalendarContract.Events.CONTENT_URI.
  • No Calendar Reading: The app opens your default calendar app with pre-filled title and details for your manual confirmation. ReceiptVault does not read, index, or harvest existing calendar events.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We respect your right to complete data autonomy:

  • Local Data Retention: Local receipts and images remain on your device indefinitely until you choose to delete individual entries or use "Delete All Data".
  • Immediate Deletion: Deleting a receipt immediately purges its SQLite database record and deletes its corresponding .jpg photo file from disk storage.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling ReceiptVault from your Android device instantly deletes all internal sandbox data, including all receipt images, the Room SQLite database, and local preferences.
  • Drive Backups: Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete backups already stored in your private Google Drive account. You can purge backups inside the app before uninstalling, or delete the ReceiptVault/backups folder directly at drive.google.com.
  • Account Deletion: Because ReceiptVault does not require or operate user accounts, there is no account to delete.
