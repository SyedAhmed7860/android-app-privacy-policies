2. Description of the Service

ReceiptVault is a native Android expense tracking and receipt management utility. Key capabilities include:

  • Camera receipt scanning via CameraX and Google Play Services Document Scanner.
  • On-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) powered by Google ML Kit.
  • Local expense categorization, budget tracking, analytics, and duplicate detection.
  • Offline-first data storage inside your Android device's private SQLite sandbox.
  • Optional cloud backup and restoration using your personal Google Drive account.
  • Exporting structured expense records to CSV, JSON, and detailed HTML reports.
