Acceptable Use Policy

Application: ReceiptVault Package Name: com.receiptvaultapp Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Purpose & Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules governing the use of the ReceiptVault mobile application (package com.receiptvaultapp). By installing or using ReceiptVault, you agree to comply with all standards set forth in this policy.

2. Permitted & Lawful Use

ReceiptVault is designed exclusively for personal bookkeeping, personal budgeting, business expense organization, and receipt archival. Permitted activities include:

  • Photographing and scanning receipts, invoices, and payment slips that you own or have authorization to retain.
  • Extracting receipt totals, tax amounts, and merchant details using on-device OCR.
  • Organizing personal and business expense records across categories.
  • Synchronizing encrypted backup archives to your personal Google Drive account.
  • Exporting structured CSV, JSON, and HTML reports for accounting and tax reconciliation.

3. Prohibited Activities

You may not use ReceiptVault, or permit any third party to use ReceiptVault, for any of the following prohibited purposes:

A. Fraudulent & Unlawful Activities

  • Scanning, forging, manipulating, or storing fraudulent financial records or counterfeit receipts to perpetrate tax fraud, insurance fraud, or employee expense reimbursement fraud.
  • Processing documents containing unlawfully obtained personal financial identifiers, stolen credit cards, or identity theft material.

B. Security Tampering & Abuse

  • Reverse engineering, decompiling, disassembling, or attempting to derive the source code of ReceiptVault, except where permitted by applicable mandatory law.
  • Circumventing or tampering with application security mechanisms, including the Google Play Billing licensing checks, developer promo verification, or App Lock controls.
  • Injecting malicious scripts, exploits, or crafted payloads into exported HTML reports or backup JSON files.
  • Abusing the Google Drive integration by attempting to flood, overwhelm, or disrupt Google cloud services.

C. Unauthorized Commercial Resale

  • Renting, leasing, sublicensing, distributing, or reselling ReceiptVault or its Pro lifetime licenses to third parties without prior written authorization.

4. Violations & Remedies

Failure to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy constitutes a material breach of our Terms of Service. In the event of a verified violation, we reserve the right to revoke Pro lifetime entitlements and terminate software licenses without liability or obligation to issue refunds.
