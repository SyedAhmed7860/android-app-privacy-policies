ReceiptVault Pro is sold exclusively as a one-time, non-consumable lifetime in-app purchase. ReceiptVault does not operate recurring subscriptions. Consequently, there are no ongoing monthly or annual charges to cancel.

1. Subscription Cancellations (Not Applicable)

Because ReceiptVault Pro does not utilize recurring subscriptions or auto-renewing billing cycles:

2. Google Play Refund Process

All financial transactions, credit card processing, and purchase settlements for ReceiptVault Pro are managed directly by Google Play. Refund eligibility is determined primarily according to Google Play's Official Refund Policies.

Within 48 Hours of Purchase:

Google Play typically handles refund requests submitted within 48 hours of purchase directly online:

  1. Open your web browser and visit Google Play Order History.
  2. Locate your ReceiptVault Pro purchase order.
  3. Click Request a refund or Report a problem.
  4. Select the reason that describes your situation and complete the form.
  5. Google typically provides an automated refund decision within 1 to 4 business days.

After 48 Hours:

If more than 48 hours have elapsed since your purchase and you experience severe technical defects or accidental duplicate billing that cannot be resolved through app updates, you may contact the developer directly for evaluation.
