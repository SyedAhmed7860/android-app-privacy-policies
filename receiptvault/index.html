Offline-First Privacy Architecture

ReceiptVault is architected with a strict local-first, privacy-by-design model. Receipt photos and financial expense data are processed entirely on your Android device using Google ML Kit on-device OCR and stored within private, sandboxed local database storage. ReceiptVault does not operate custom backend servers or cloud databases.
