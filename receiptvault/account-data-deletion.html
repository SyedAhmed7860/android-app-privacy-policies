Data Deletion & Account Information

Application: ReceiptVault Package Name: com.receiptvaultapp Compliance: Google Play User Data Policy
Notice on Account Creation & Google Play Policy

ReceiptVault does not feature an account creation system. You are never asked to register, create a username, set a password, or maintain a cloud profile. Because no developer-managed accounts exist, Google Play's "App Account Deletion" URL requirement is technically not applicable. You retain complete, direct physical control over your data on your Android device.

1. How to Delete Your Receipt Data

You have full autonomy to delete your data at any time using the following options:

Option A: Delete an Individual Receipt

  1. Open ReceiptVault on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Receipts tab and tap on the receipt you wish to remove.
  3. Tap the Delete (trash can) icon in the top app bar.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt.

Technical Effect: The receipt record and all associated line items are permanently deleted from the local Room SQLite database. If a receipt photo was saved, the physical .jpg file is permanently erased from internal storage (context.filesDir/receipts) immediately.

Option B: Erase All Local Data (Nuclear Option)

  1. Open ReceiptVault.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Scroll to the Data & Privacy section and tap Delete All Data.
  4. Confirm the warning dialog.

Technical Effect: The application executes a complete recursive purge of the receipts directory on internal storage, wipes all rows from all Room SQLite tables (receipts, line items, backup metadata), and resets local preference flags.

Option C: Delete Data via Android Operating System

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps > ReceiptVault.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

Technical Effect: The Android operating system immediately destroys all data within the application's private sandbox directory, restoring the app to its fresh install state.

2. How to Delete Google Drive Backups

If you enabled optional Google Drive backups, backup snapshots reside in your personal Google Drive account under the ReceiptVault/backups folder. You can remove them using either method:

Method 1: From Inside ReceiptVault

  1. Open ReceiptVault > Settings.
  2. Under Cloud Backup, tap Disconnect Google Drive.
  3. Confirm disconnection. The app clears its cached Google OAuth session token immediately.

Method 2: Directly via Google Drive (Web or Mobile)

  1. Open Google Drive in your web browser or app.
  2. Search for or navigate to the ReceiptVault folder.
  3. Select the backups folder (or specific .json snapshot files) and select Delete / Move to Trash.
  4. Empty your Google Drive Trash to permanently purge the files.

3. What Happens When You Uninstall ReceiptVault

  • Local Data: Completely destroyed. Android OS automatically deletes the entire private data directory (/data/user/0/com.receiptvaultapp), including all receipt photos and the SQLite database.
  • Google Drive Backups: Remain safely inside your personal Google Drive until you manually delete them or overwrite them. ReceiptVault loses all ability to interact with your Drive upon uninstallation.
  • Pro Purchases: Your lifetime Pro purchase entitlement is tied to your Google Play account (Google Account). If you reinstall ReceiptVault in the future, your Pro license will restore automatically via Google Play Billing.
