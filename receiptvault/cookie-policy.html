Explicit Zero-Tracking Declaration

This website does not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or tracking. You will never encounter cookie consent banners or tracking scripts on this documentation portal.

1. Policy Website (Static Portal)

This legal and documentation website is delivered strictly as a static web resource. We do not use:

  • First-party cookies or session cookies.
  • Third-party advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • Web analytics trackers (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar, Facebook Pixel).
  • Browser fingerprinting or local storage tracking scripts.

Your web browser may temporarily cache standard assets (such as CSS, vanilla JS, and our brand logo) locally on your device solely to improve page load speed.

2. ReceiptVault Android Mobile Application

The ReceiptVault Android application (com.receiptvaultapp) is a 100% native Android mobile application built with Jetpack Compose and Kotlin:

  • The app does not utilize embedded WebViews to display user interfaces.
  • The app does not store or process HTTP browser cookies.
  • Local user preferences (such as selected currency, dark/light theme, and PIN hash) are maintained using native Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences stored securely in the app's internal filesystem.

3. Third-Party Advertising (AdMob) Note

While ReceiptVault does not use web cookies, users on the free tier of the Android application may see advertisements served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). AdMob operates through mobile platform identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID) rather than web cookies. For information on managing your mobile advertising ID, see our Privacy Policy.
