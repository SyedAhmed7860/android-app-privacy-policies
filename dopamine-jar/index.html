01. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application The Dopamine Jar (hereinafter referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), designed for Android devices.

The Dopamine Jar is a mindful, contemplative application created to offer users meaningful reflections, gentle comfort, and positive emotional reminders through an interactive digital glass apothecary jar. We believe personal reflection and emotional wellness require complete intimacy and discretion. Therefore, our application is engineered so that your personal thoughts, reflections, and usage patterns never leave your physical mobile device.

03. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike many mobile applications, The Dopamine Jar does not require or request network connectivity. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not collect personal identification data: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identifiers.
  • Does not require account registration or login: You can use all features immediately upon installation without creating a profile or authenticating.
  • Does not collect device or network identifiers: No IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, Advertising ID (AAID), or IP address.
  • Does not collect location data: No GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular network location tracking.
  • Does not request network permissions: The application manifest (AndroidManifest.xml) does not declare android.permission.INTERNET or android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. The Android operating system physically prevents the App from establishing internet connections.

04. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data generated during your use of the App is stored strictly in the private application sandbox allocated to com.dopaminejar.app on your device's internal storage:

A. Journal Entries & Personal Reflections

When you reflect on a note, the App allows you to record an emotional reaction (such as "Needed this", "Made me think", or "Not today") and optional freeform personal text. This information is saved solely in an encrypted-at-rest local SQLite database via Android Jetpack Room (dopamine_jar_database). It is never synchronized to cloud servers or accessible by third parties.

B. Saved Notes & Favorites

When you mark a note as a favorite (❤️), that preference and the timestamp of discovery are recorded in the local database on your device so you can revisit them in your personal collection.

C. Streaks & Mindfulness Activity

To encourage mindful daily habits without intrusive pressure, the App locally counts consecutive days of usage and records the date of your last interaction (e.g. 2026-10-03). This counter is saved in Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences.

D. User Settings & Aesthetic Preferences

Your chosen application preferences are saved locally in Jetpack DataStore Preferences, including:

  • Sound effects toggle (enabled/disabled)
  • Haptic feedback toggle (enabled/disabled)
  • Daily reminder notification preferences and scheduled time (hour and minute)
  • Jar material selection (Classic Crystal, Amber Apothecary, Frosted Sea Glass, Smoked Obsidian, Rose Quartz)
  • Onboarding completion flag

05. Device Hardware & Sensor Usage

The App uses specific device hardware capabilities to deliver an interactive physical experience:

A. Accelerometer & Motion Sensors

The App utilizes your device's built-in accelerometer (Sensor.TYPE_ACCELEROMETER) via the Android SensorManager system service for two specific real-time purposes:

  1. 3D Parallax Tilt: Measuring subtle device pitch and roll to dynamically adjust glass light reflections and visual depth of paper layers inside the jar.
  2. Physical Shake Gesture: Detecting when you physically shake your phone to trigger the jar-shaking ceremony hands-free.
🔒 Sensor Privacy Guarantee

Accelerometer data is processed in volatile device memory in real-time, filtered via a mathematical low-pass filter, and discarded immediately after the frame renders. Sensor readings are never recorded, stored on disk, logged, or transmitted.

B. Haptic Vibrator (android.permission.VIBRATE)

The App requests android.permission.VIBRATE to provide subtle physical tactile feedback (such as paper rustling clicks, glass tap sensations, and settling thuds) using Android's Vibrator / VibratorManager. No user data is accessed, collected, or processed through the vibration subsystem.

06. Audio & Haptic Functionality

The App features a custom ASMR sound engine that produces soothing auditory feedback during jar interactions:

  • Synthetic Audio Generation: All sound effects (glass tap, paper rustle, 528Hz meditative chime, paper unfold) are synthesized mathematically in memory using raw PCM audio waveforms via Android's native AudioTrack API.
  • No Audio Recording: The App does not access, request, or use the microphone. No audio is ever recorded, captured, or analyzed.
  • User Control: Sound effects can be toggled on or off at any time within the App's settings screen.

07. Notifications & Background Tasks

The App includes an optional daily reminder notification feature:

  • Local Scheduled Alarms: Reminders are scheduled locally on the device using Android's native AlarmManager system service.
  • Notification Permission (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13 (API level 33) and above, the App asks for your explicit permission before posting notifications.
  • Reboot Restoration (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): If your device restarts, a lightweight BroadcastReceiver restores your chosen local reminder schedule.
  • No Remote Push Services: We do not operate or use external push notification servers (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging or OneSignal). All notification triggers originate 100% locally from your device's internal clock.

08. Home Screen Widget

The App provides an optional Android Home Screen App Widget (DopamineJarWidgetProvider). The widget reads daily inspiring text from the pre-bundled offline quote database and presents it on your home screen. The widget runs entirely on-device through Android's AppWidgetManager, does not track interactions, and involves no background network communication.

09. User-Initiated Content Sharing

When reading an inspiring note, you have the option to share it with friends, family, or other apps:

  • Standard Android Share Sheet: Sharing is entirely voluntary and initiates via Android's standard Intent.ACTION_SEND system chooser.
  • Temporary Image Sharing via FileProvider: If you choose to share a note as a visual card graphic, the App creates a temporary image file in your device's cache and grants temporary, read-only URI access to the recipient application selected by you (e.g. messaging or social apps) using Android's secure androidx.core.content.FileProvider.
  • No Tracking: We do not track what you share, when you share, or which recipient app you select.

10. Third-Party SDKs, Analytics & Advertising

We strongly respect your digital peace and privacy. Consequently:

  • No Advertising SDKs: The App contains no advertisements, banner ads, interstitial videos, or commercial tracking networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or Meta Audience Network).
  • No Analytics SDKs: The App contains no behavioral analytics, telemetry, or user event tracking libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or Flurry).
  • No Remote Crash Reporting SDKs: The App does not send automatic crash reports or stack traces to external servers.
  • No Social Media SDKs: No third-party social logins, trackers, or SDKs are embedded in the App.

13. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is retained exclusively on your physical device, you maintain absolute control over data retention and permanent deletion:

  • Deleting Individual Entries: You can delete any saved note or reflection entry individually at any time from within the App's Collection and Journal screens.
  • Clearing All Application Data: You can permanently wipe all journal entries, saved notes, streaks, and preferences by navigating to your device's system settings:
    Settings > Apps > The Dopamine Jar > Storage > Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
  • Uninstalling the Application: Uninstalling the App permanently removes the application sandbox directory (/data/data/com.dopaminejar.app), deleting 100% of stored notes, journal reflections, and settings. Because no data exists on external servers, this deletion is instantaneous and irreversible.

14. Data Security

The security of your thoughts and reflections is protected by the foundational architecture of the Android Operating System:

  • Application Sandboxing: Android isolates each app in its own distinct Linux process with private file permissions. Other applications installed on your device cannot access The Dopamine Jar's database or preferences.
  • Air-Gapped Isolation: Because the App does not request internet access permissions, it is structurally impossible for malicious code or unauthorized actors to exfiltrate your reflections over the network.
  • Device Encryption: On modern Android devices, all app sandbox data is automatically encrypted at rest by Android's Full Disk Encryption (FDE) or File-Based Encryption (FBE).
