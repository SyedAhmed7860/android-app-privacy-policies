Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 Application: DocuPDF – Scanner & PDF Tools Version: 1.0

Core Privacy Commitment: DocuPDF is built on a local-first architecture. Your documents, photos, scanned pages, and extracted text never leave your device and are never uploaded to any DocuPDF server or cloud storage.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DocuPDF (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application, DocuPDF: Scanner & PDF Tools (the “App”), available on Google Play.

We believe document scanners handle your most sensitive personal and business information: invoices, contracts, receipts, identity documents, and personal notes. We designed DocuPDF to function completely offline without user accounts, cloud databases, or backend servers.

2. Architecture & Local Processing

DocuPDF operates with the following privacy-first technical principles:

  • No User Account: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email, or telephone number to use DocuPDF in Version 1.
  • No Backend Server: DocuPDF does not operate any backend server, remote database, or cloud synchronization service for user files.
  • Local Document Processing: All scanning, edge detection, perspective correction, color enhancement filters, PDF merging, splitting, compression, and password encryption occur strictly on your device's local hardware and memory.
  • Local OCR (Optical Character Recognition): Text extraction is performed entirely on-device using local machine learning libraries. Your scanned documents and extracted text are never transmitted over the internet for processing.

3. Device Permissions & Data Handling

To provide core scanner and PDF functionality, the App requests only standard Android permissions when required:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Camera
android.permission.CAMERA		 Used solely when you tap “Scan Document” to capture photos of physical documents. Live camera frames are processed in-memory. Only the photos you explicitly capture are stored in the App's private local storage. No video or audio is ever recorded or transmitted.
Storage / Media Access
Standard Android File Picker		 Used when you choose to import existing images or PDF documents from your device, or save an exported PDF to your device. DocuPDF uses the modern Android Storage Access Framework. The App only accesses the specific files you select. It does not scan, index, or inspect your unrelated photos or personal folders.
Internet Access
android.permission.INTERNET		 Used exclusively by Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements in the free tier, and by Google Play Billing to verify purchase entitlements. The DocuPDF app itself never transmits your documents, metadata, or personal files over the internet. Network traffic is restricted strictly to Google SDK communications.

4. Third-Party Services

DocuPDF integrates limited, reputable third-party services from Google LLC to provide monetization and store purchase validation:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising in Free Version)

For users using the free version of DocuPDF, advertisements may be displayed via Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-sensitive diagnostic and advertising data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID).
  • General device information (device model, operating system version, carrier, language).
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks).
  • Coarse IP address (used for geographic ad targeting and fraud prevention).

Ad-Free Experience: If you upgrade to DocuPDF Lifetime Pro, advertisements are permanently disabled, and ad requests are discontinued.

To review Google's privacy practices, please visit:
• Google Privacy Policy
• How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

B. Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

DocuPDF offers an optional one-time “Lifetime Pro” in-app purchase. All transactions are handled directly through Google Play Billing.

  • DocuPDF does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses.
  • Google Play processes all payments according to the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • The App only receives anonymous purchase confirmation tokens from Google Play to verify that your Pro license is active.

5. Information We Do NOT Collect

To avoid any ambiguity, DocuPDF explicitly does NOT collect, sell, lease, or transmit:

  • The contents or images of documents you scan.
  • Text extracted via Optical Character Recognition (OCR).
  • Names, folder names, or file sizes of your personal documents.
  • Passwords applied to protected PDF documents.
  • Signatures created or placed inside documents.
  • Contacts, location coordinates, microphone input, or SMS history.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all documents and settings are stored locally on your device:

  • User Control: You can delete any scan, page, or document at any time from within the App. Once deleted, the file is removed from your local storage immediately.
  • App Uninstallation: If you uninstall DocuPDF or clear the App's data via Android Settings, all documents stored in the App's private internal storage will be permanently erased. Please export your necessary PDFs to your device's external storage before uninstalling.

7. Data Security

We employ industry-standard Android security practices to protect your information:

  • Documents are maintained in Android scoped app-private directories inaccessible to other standard apps.
  • PDF password encryption uses standard PDF security algorithms executed locally on your device.
  • No transmission of document payloads takes place across public or private networks.

8. Children’s Privacy

DocuPDF is a general audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through third-party services within the App, please contact us.

9. User Rights & Choices

Depending on your region (including GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA/CPRA in California), you have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Deletion: Your documents are fully under your custody on your physical device. You may access, export, or delete them at any time directly through the app interface.
  • Advertising ID Reset / Opt-Out: You may reset or opt-out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • Pro Ad Removal: You may purchase the Lifetime Pro license to permanently stop third-party ad network requests.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our application or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be indicated by an updated “Effective Date” at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
